# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First release. Galaxy Buds2 support with battery, noise controls, ambient sound,
equaliser, touch settings, custom touch-and-hold commands, find my earbuds, rename,
firmware updates, diagnostics, head tracking, a dark menu bar panel, Now Playing for Apple
Music and Spotify, launch at login, and launch when the earbuds connect. Decoders for
other Galaxy Buds generations are included but untested.
